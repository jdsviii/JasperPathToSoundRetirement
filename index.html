Monte Carlo runs 1,000 random market sequences using your expected mean return + volatility to model sequence-of-returns risk (like FIREcalc historical testing).
Deterministic uses a fixed return each year for a single, smooth projection path (no randomness).
Volatility % is the expected year-to-year fluctuation (standard deviation) of portfolio returns. Higher volatility (e.g. 15–20%) creates wider outcome ranges and more uncertainty; lower volatility produces more stable paths. The default of 15% approximates a typical diversified stock portfolio.
Chart path meanings:
  • 10th Percentile — Tough market sequence (only 10% of outcomes were worse)
  • 25th Percentile — Below-average sequence
  • Median (50th) — Typical / middle outcome (half of simulations did better, half worse)
  • 75th Percentile — Above-average sequence
  • 90th Percentile — Strong market sequence (only 10% of outcomes were better)
