From pressure to peace

2027 Path Calendar

Five calm moves a month, twelve months in a row

Path to Sound Retirement
pathtosoundretirement.com

Do each month's moves in the month they belong, and the year stops feeling like pressure. The free calculators and Learning stay free; use them to turn worry into a plan you can follow.

months with hard deadlines

January (has hard deadlines)

Begin
  • Q4 estimated tax payment for 2026: Jan 15 (if you pay estimates)
  • Missed Medicare sign-up? General Enrollment opens Jan 1
  • Review beneficiaries on every account and policy
  • Write down what "enough" looks like for 2027
  • Run the free Retirement Calculator (Monte Carlo tests many market paths) with honest inputs

February

Gather
  • Save year-end statements for every account in one folder
  • Check each tax form that arrives against your own records
  • Read your success rate: how often the plan lasted in the test
  • Emergency fund check: could it cover a surprise this month?
  • List every subscription and recurring bill; cancel what no longer serves you

March (has hard deadlines)

Prepare
  • Medicare General Enrollment closes Mar 31
  • Finish gathering tax paperwork well before Apr 15
  • If eligible, decide on a 2026 IRA or HSA contribution (due Apr 15)
  • Retiring before Medicare? Map your health coverage
  • Confirm your RMD (required yearly withdrawal) starting age; it depends on your birth year (IRS.gov, your advisor)

April (has hard deadlines)

File
  • Reaching your RMD age in 2026? Your first required withdrawal is due Apr 1
  • Federal filing deadline: Apr 15; an extension does not extend payment
  • Q1 estimated tax payment for 2027: Apr 15 (if you pay estimates)
  • Last day for 2026 IRA and HSA contributions: Apr 15
  • File, then save a copy of your return with your records

May

Verify
  • Sign in to my Social Security and review each year of earnings
  • Compare real spending with the spending you entered in the calculators
  • Check your investment mix against your plan; rebalance only if it drifted
  • Pick one Learning article on a worry and read it this month
  • Find the yearly fee on each fund you own

June (has hard deadlines)

Midyear
  • Q2 estimated tax payment: Jun 15 (if you pay estimates)
  • Pressure-to-peace check-in: what feels heavy, and what is one step?
  • Project this year's income and taxes while you can still adjust
  • Update calculator inputs with your real balances
  • Plan one real rest this summer; the plan exists to serve your life

July

Steady
  • Change one lever at a time: spending, return, or timing
  • Check your credit reports, free every week at AnnualCreditReport.com
  • Walk someone you trust through your plan and invite questions
  • Run a hard case: weak markets early. Write what you would do
  • Celebrate one thing your money did for you this year

August

Guard
  • Set spending guardrails: preset rules for when to trim or raise spending
  • Write your rules in plain words while markets are calm
  • Size your cash buffer: money set aside so a bad market never forces a sale
  • Ask when employer open enrollment starts; many hold it in the fall
  • Draft your open enrollment questions before the window opens

September (has hard deadlines)

Align
  • Q3 estimated tax payment: Sep 15 (if you pay estimates)
  • Open enrollment at work: read the options early and note the deadline
  • On Medicare? Review your current plan before Open Enrollment (Oct 15)
  • Book time for your annual retirement plan review
  • Deciding when to claim Social Security? Work through the checklist

October (has hard deadlines)

Choose
  • Medicare Open Enrollment opens Oct 15; changes start Jan 1
  • Extended filing deadline: Oct 15 (if you filed for an extension)
  • Make your employer open enrollment choices, then confirm they went through
  • Start your RMD plan: which accounts, how much, and when
  • Rerun the calculator with fall balances and compare to January

November

Simplify
  • Giving from an IRA? A QCD (qualified charitable distribution) pays the charity directly
  • Ask each custodian how long year-end transfers take
  • Confirm your RMD amount for each account
  • Tell your family where your documents and logins live
  • Close or merge one account you no longer need

December (has hard deadlines)

Finish
  • Medicare Open Enrollment closes Dec 7; make your choice
  • RMD deadline: Dec 31; a first-year RMD may wait until next spring
  • QCD must be completed by Dec 31
  • Hold your annual retirement plan review and save the notes
  • Close the year with one more pressure-to-peace check-in

Education, not advice. Dates shift with weekends and holidays - confirm anything that affects your money with the IRS, SSA, Medicare, or a qualified professional.

Optional coaching is there if you want to learn the path with someone. Never required.

Dates checked: October 2026. Deadlines on the sheet come from IRS, Medicare, and Social Security pages as they read that month. Weekends and holidays can shift a date, and your situation decides which ones apply to you, so confirm before you act.

The reminders file (.ics) lists only the hard dates, as all-day events with alerts one week and one day ahead. It works with Google Calendar, Apple Calendar, and Outlook. Want the story behind the sheet? Read How to Use Your 2027 Path Calendar.
