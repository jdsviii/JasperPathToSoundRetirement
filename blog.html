Recommended Reading

These books provide deeper insight into the principles behind long-term wealth building and retirement planning.

The Simple Path to Wealth

by JL Collins

The clearest and most motivating guide to index fund investing and financial independence. Highly recommended for anyone who wants a worry-free approach.

The Total Money Makeover

by Dave Ramsey

A practical, step-by-step plan for getting out of debt, building an emergency fund, and creating the financial foundation that makes long-term investing possible. Ramsey focuses on behavior and self-control - the habits that turn good intentions into lasting progress.

View on Amazon

Money for Couples

by Ramit Sethi

A practical guide for partners who want fewer money fights and a shared plan. Focuses on values, communication, and building systems together so both people feel heard and the household moves in the same direction.

View on Amazon

I Will Teach You to Be Rich

by Ramit Sethi

A practical six-week program for automating your finances, crushing debt, and spending on what you value - without guilt. Especially useful for building systems so contributions and savings happen without relying on willpower alone.

View on Amazon

The Psychology of Money

by Morgan Housel

Timeless lessons on wealth, greed, and happiness. Housel shows why behavior matters more than spreadsheets - and how ordinary people build extraordinary results by staying calm, patient, and consistent over decades.
