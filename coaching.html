Virtual Financial Coaching

From pressure to peace - with a clear path and real accountability.

The calculator and articles on this site are free. Coaching is for people who want more than information. It is for people who are ready to own their plan and want a teacher-hearted guide to help them see the path and stay with it.

This is not about someone managing everything for you. It is about clarity, a written strategy, and optional support while you do the work.

Who this is for

  • You want clarity on your goals and a practical strategy to reach them
  • You are willing to do the work - you are not looking for someone to manage everything for you
  • You value accountability, honest feedback, and learning as you go
  • You prefer a teacher’s heart over a sales pitch or a black-box “plan”

Who this is not for

  • People who want someone else to make all the decisions
  • Anyone seeking personalized investment recommendations or portfolio management
  • Those looking for a one-time “fix” without follow-through

The 3-Phase Path

1

Clarity Conversation

We meet virtually and build a high-level picture of where you are. The focus is on your goals - what “enough” and “peace” actually look like for you. Numbers support the conversation; they do not drive it alone.

2

Your Written Path

You receive a clear, written strategy that connects your current situation to your goals. It is prioritized, practical, and designed so you can follow it. This is your map, not a document that sits in a drawer.

3

Execution Support (optional)

You execute the plan. If you want ongoing accountability and guidance while you do the work, we can walk through it together. The ownership stays with you. The support is there when you want it.

How it connects to the free tools

Coaching clients are encouraged to use the free calculator, checklists, and articles as part of the process. The free resources stay free. Coaching adds personal clarity, a written strategy, and optional accountability.

How coaching works with the free tools

The calculator, Learning articles, and checklists on this site stay free. Coaching does not replace them. It is for people who want a live conversation, a written path tailored to their goals, and optional accountability while they execute.

Many households only need the free tools: honest inputs, a Monte Carlo stress test, and a few articles on success rates, sequence risk, and spending. Coaching is the next step when you already know you will do the work - and you want a teacher-hearted partner for clarity and follow-through.

You will still enter your own numbers, make your own decisions, and live with the plan. I will not manage your portfolio or pick securities for you. I will help you see tradeoffs clearly and stay accountable to the path you choose.

What you walk away with

  • A clearer statement of goals and constraints - not vague wishes
  • A written path from your current financial picture toward those goals
  • Priorities you can execute without needing a new strategy every month
  • Optional execution support if you want accountability after the plan is written

What coaching is not

Coaching here is educational and accountability-focused. It is not discretionary portfolio management, tax preparation, or legal advice. It is not a promise of investment returns. The free calculator remains the place to stress-test numbers; coaching is the place to clarify decisions and follow through.

If you need a fiduciary investment advisor, an accountant, or an attorney, those roles are separate. I will encourage you to use the right professional when a question falls outside teaching and planning accountability.

Before you book a Discovery call

Try the calculator with honest inputs. Read one or two Learning articles that match your situation - for example success rates, sequence risk, or budgeting for retirement readiness. Notice where you feel stuck: numbers, habits, or follow-through. Bring that observation to the call. Discovery is free and exists to test fit, not to pressure a sale.

If the free tools already give you a clear path and you are executing, you may not need coaching - and that is a good outcome. From pressure to peace does not require a paid relationship. It requires a plan you understand and actions you take.
