Pricing FAQ

Is the discovery call really free?

Yes. It is a short conversation to confirm goals and fit. There is no charge and no obligation to buy a package.

How do I pay?

Payment details are shared privately after the discovery call, once you decide to move forward with a package. Options typically include PayPal or Venmo. Public payment handles are not listed on this site.

What is the difference between Clarity Path and Full Path?

Clarity Path covers Phases 1 and 2: a goals-focused conversation and a written strategy you can execute on your own. Full Path adds Phase 3: structured check-in sessions and accountability while you implement the plan.

Who is Execution Support Only for?

People who already completed Clarity Path, or who already have a clear written plan and only want accountability while they execute. It does not include a new Clarity Conversation or a new Written Path.

Can I pay in installments?

Sometimes, by agreement. If a payment plan would make a package workable for you, mention it on the discovery call. Any plan is confirmed in writing before work begins.

What if I am not sure which package fits?

Start with the free discovery call. Part of that conversation is choosing the lightest package that still meets your goals. Many people begin with Clarity Path and add Execution Support later if they want it.

Do you offer refunds?

Discovery calls are free. For paid packages, refund policy is confirmed when you enroll. In general, work already delivered (for example, a completed Written Path) is not refundable; unused future check-in sessions may be handled case by case.

Is this financial or investment advice?

No. This coaching is educational and accountability-focused. It is not financial, investment, tax, or legal advice. You remain responsible for your own decisions. For advice specific to your situation, consult qualified professionals.
